# Who Where When

Guess who took the photo, where, and when

## The game

Friends join a lobby and upload photos from their camera rolls. Then the game starts.

Each round shows one photo. You guess three things:

- **Who** uploaded it
- **Where** it was taken, as a pin on the map
- **When** it was taken

You score on each, closer is better. Location and time come from the photo's metadata, so there's nothing to fill in.
