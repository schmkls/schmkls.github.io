# Hit Market

Trade songs like stocks, priced by plays

## The game

Invest in songs on Spotify. Your return is how much a song's plays grow relative to where they were when you bought in, not the raw count. A song going from 1,000 to 10,000 plays beats a hit going from 1 billion to 1.1 billion.

The benchmark is the market: the average growth of all songs. Beat it, or you'd have done better holding the index.

## Trading

Players buy and sell song shares to each other at whatever price they agree on. Spot a song before it blows up, buy it cheap from a friend who doesn't believe in it, and sell when everyone else does.
