# Viewing Route

Fit more home viewings into one day

## The Problem

Looking for a home means going to viewings, and viewings take time. They are short, spread across the city, and often overlap — so you end up seeing fewer homes than you could.

## The Solution

Paste a link to your saved filter on [Hemnet](https://www.hemnet.se/) or [Booli](https://www.booli.se/), pick a day, and get a route that fits in as many viewings as possible — with travel time between them accounted for.

For anyone buying a home who wants each round of viewings to count.
