# StatGuessr

Guess the number, score by how close you were

## The game

Every round asks for a number. You type (or drag on a slider) your guess, the real value is revealed, and you score based on how far off you were.

> How many people live in Germany?\
> You: 80,000,000
> Actual: 83,500,000\
> 4.2% off → 9,580 / 10,000

Questions get progressively harder within a game — from the population of Germany to the share of animal-caused deaths that are hippos.

Sources are shown for each question. 

## Why it works

Like GeoGuessr and Wordle, one daily challenge is enough to build a habit and something to compare with friends. Over time you get stats on which areas you have the best feel for.
